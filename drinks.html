    How the estimate works

    The estimate uses a simplified Widmark formula: alcohol grams divided by body weight and the selected distribution factor, with an assumed elimination rate of 0.015 percentage points per hour. It assumes instant absorption and applies elimination between logged drinks. The displayed value is rounded upward to three decimal places.

    Actual BAC can differ substantially or rise after your last drink. Food, medications, body composition, metabolism, incomplete entries, and other factors are not reliably accounted for. The 0.08% warning is not a legal or safety clearance.

    Estimates and attention questions cannot determine whether you are safe to drive.
